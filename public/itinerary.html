Drag activities, places to stay and restaurants from the left onto a day (or tap +). Choose each day's island and the travel between islands appears automatically, with door-to-door times and costs. The plan assumes you eat hotel-included meals at the hotel; drop restaurants onto breakfast, lunch or dinner to plan the rest, and any meal left open is counted at the allowance below.
